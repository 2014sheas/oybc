Privacy Policy Terms of Use

The short version

  • Your boards and tasks live on your device first. When you're signed in, we copy them to our cloud database (Google Firebase) so they sync between your devices.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We don't sell your information.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time. Your synced data is then deleted from our servers.
  • Reminders are set up on your iPhone and delivered by iOS. We don't run a push server.

2. What we collect

Account information

You can use OYBC with an account or as a guest.

  • Email and password: your email address. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication (run by Google) and never reaches our database.
  • Sign in with Apple: the email address on your Apple ID — or a private relay address if you choose Hide My Email — and your name, if you choose to share it.
  • Sign in with Google: the email address, name and profile photo link on your Google account.
  • Guest: an anonymous account with a random ID and no email or name. If you save your account later, your guest data stays with it.
  • Display name: the name you set on your profile, if any.

Firebase Authentication also keeps basic account records, such as when the account was created, when it last signed in, and which sign-in methods are linked.

What you put in OYBC

Your boards, tasks, sub-tasks, counters, task pools, repeating boards and board defaults; each completion or count you log, with its time; and your preferences (theme, week start, board defaults and notification settings, including your reminder time). This is the content of the app, and we use it only to run OYBC for you.

Your profile record

Your account has one profile record: your account ID, email address, display name, profile photo link (from Google, if any), your preferences, and when the record was created and last updated. It syncs like the rest of your data.

The oybc.com launch list

If you join the launch list on oybc.com, we store your email address, where you signed up (for example, the Coming Soon page), when you signed up, and whether you've unsubscribed. We use it to send you a one-time confirmation and to tell you when OYBC launches. We also receive an email notice of each new signup.

Technical information

Like any online service, our hosting and database provider (Google Firebase) processes the technical information needed to handle requests — such as IP addresses, device or browser type and request times — and may keep it in service logs. We don't use it to profile or track you. The oybc.com website and the OYBC web app load their fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when it fetches them.

What we don't collect

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools. We don't track you across other apps or websites, and the app doesn't use your device's advertising identifier.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or health data.
  • No access to your photo library (see Photos below).
  • We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

3. Permissions on iPhone

  • Notifications (off until you turn them on): reminders — a board about to end, a new week, month or year starting, a daily reminder at a time you pick — are scheduled by the app on your iPhone while it's open, and iOS delivers them. There's no push-notification server; the only reminder information we store is the notification settings in your preferences.
  • Photos (add only): when you save a share poster, OYBC asks to add images to your photo library. It can only add the poster; it can't see or read your photos.
  • Sharing: a share poster is made on your device and goes only where you send it. What happens after that is up to the app or service you choose.

4. How we use your information

  • To run OYBC: sign you in, and store and sync your data across your devices.
  • To keep your account secure: Firebase Authentication sends password-reset and email-change emails on our behalf, and our database rules let only your signed-in account read or write your data.
  • To answer you when you contact us.
  • To send launch-list emails, if you joined the list.

If you're in the European Economic Area or the UK, our legal bases are: providing the service you asked for (account and app data); your consent (launch-list emails — you can withdraw it by unsubscribing); and our legitimate interest in running a secure service (technical and security information).

5. Where your data lives

  • On your device. OYBC keeps your data in a database on your device (inside the app on iPhone; in your browser's storage on the web), so it works offline. The web app also keeps a few display settings in your browser's local storage.
  • In the cloud. When you're signed in (guests included), your data is copied to Google Cloud Firestore under your account and synced in the background. Our Firebase project stores data in Google Cloud's United States multi-region, and our server functions run in the United States. If you live elsewhere, your data is transferred to the US.   • Security. Data travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and our database rules restrict each account's data to that account. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

7. How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • We keep your account and synced data for as long as you have an account.
  • When you delete a board or task, it's marked as deleted rather than erased, so your other devices remove it too. Deleted items stay in your synced data, hidden, until your account is deleted.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Account & security → Delete account (iPhone and web). As a guest, use Settings → Discard guest data. This deletes your sign-in account; our server then deletes all of your synced data from our database, and the app erases its local database on that device. We keep one record — your account's internal ID and the time it was deleted — so a device that's still signed in can't upload data again afterwards.
  • Signing out doesn't erase the data stored on that device. Data on another device you used stays there until you delete the app or clear the browser's site data.
  • Technical logs held by our providers are deleted on those providers' schedules.
  • Launch list: we keep it until we've announced the launch [OWNER: confirm how long the list is kept after launch]. If you unsubscribe, we keep your address marked as unsubscribed so we don't email you again. Email us to have it erased completely.
